# Basic definitions

**Metrology** in its broadest definition, is the science of measurement

**Sensor** is a device that measures/detects a signal or stimulus to
acquire information from the real world.

**Measurement** is the process of comparing an *unknown* quantity
with an accepted *standard* of the same quantity.

for the measuring process to be done, there has to be the following:
- measured quentity
- Standard system
- measuring instruments
- measuring technology

## Transducer
- a device that converts a signal from one physical form to a corresponding signal having a different physical form
- they are also energy converters or modifiers
- *i.e*: mechanical, thermal, magnetic, electric, optical, chemical

an input transducer is called *Sensor*
an output transducer is called *Actuator*

# introduction to sensors and transducers
we already know that a transducer is a device that converts energy from one form to another.
we also know sensors and actuators are types of transducers

### sensor
A device that measures/detects a signal or stimulus to acquire information from the real world

in sensors, we are more interested in *Reading* it's data input
*i.e* when using a microphone, we are interested in taking the signal input (the sound wave) to process it

### Actuators
a device that generates a signal or stimulus to affect the state of the real world

sensors get data from the real world while actuators have an affect on it
*i.e* when using a speaker, we are interested in outputting a desired signal (an amplified sound wave) from a modified input signal
